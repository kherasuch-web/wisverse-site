Privacy Policy

WisVerse (Wisdom Universe) · Effective 18 November 2026

In short

WisVerse does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your files, notes, highlights, quotes and settings are kept on your own device.

What stays on your device

PDF files you open, files the app creates (saved in Documents/WisVerse), your marks and notes, bookmarks, reading positions, quotes, your signature, your Family Mode PIN (stored only as a hash) and app settings. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

Permissions and why

PermissionUsed for
All files accessTo list the PDF files on your phone in the library, and to delete a file only when you ask. Files are read on the phone only.
InternetOnly when you tap Download for a text-reading (OCR) language file. The file comes from a public GitHub address and is checked after download. Nothing about you or your books is sent.

Camera scanning

Scanning uses Google's document scanner, which is part of Google Play services on your phone. The pictures are processed on the phone and handed to WisVerse; WisVerse does not upload them. Google Play services may send Google basic diagnostic and usage information about the scanner, under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Family Mode

Family Mode checks page text and pictures with a word list and a small picture model that run entirely on the phone. Nothing is uploaded and no results are kept outside the app.

Backups

If your phone's own Google backup is turned on, Android may save the app's small settings file (bookmarks, quotes, notes, settings) to your Google account, encrypted with your screen lock. PDF files are not included. This backup is run by Android and Google, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings. You can also save a backup file yourself from the app and keep it wherever you choose.

Sharing

When you choose to share or save a file to another app, that app's own privacy policy applies to what you send it.

Children

WisVerse collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
